# Collage pack: sources and licences

Everything in `art/packs/collage` is cut from these files by `tools/make_collage_pack.py`.
Only public-domain or CC0 images. Mobs (Slayme, the Pool slimes, Novecento, Ada, Bit, Gig) are not here:
they stay hand-made in the placeholder pack.

## Paintings (public domain: the painters died in the 16th-17th century)
Low-resolution reproductions from the WikiArt subset in
github.com/diazmarteth/Emotion-aware-recommendation-over-WikiArt-ArtEmis (`wikiart_256/Northern_Renaissance`).
Faithful photographs of 2D public-domain works carry no new copyright.

- Pieter Bruegel the Elder: Children's Games (1560), Haymaking (1565), The Hunters in the Snow (1565),
  The Land of Cockaigne (1567), The Peasant Wedding (1568), Summer (1568, drawing), The Fat Kitchen (1563, engraving),
  The Gloomy Day (1559), The Hare Hunt (1560, etching), The Triumph of Death, Wooded Region (1566, engraving)
- Village street with a windmill (`village-street.jpg`, Brueghel family, 17th century)
